Get help if you're struggling to repay benefit money you owe

If you're struggling to repay benefit money you owe, you can choose a payment plan that's right for you.

There is also help and support available from the Department of Work and Pensions (DWP) Debt Management team and independent organisations.

How the DWP Debt Management team can help

If you're struggling to repay benefit money you owe for any reason, let us know. Our agents will work with you, providing help and support with your repayments.

Depending on your circumstances, they may offer help and support including:

  • adjusting how much you pay each month to make it affordable
  • setting up a short break from repaying the money you owe
  • getting a free, independant debt advisor to contact you
  • telling you about organisations that can give you additional support
If you are struggling to repay an advance you got on your current Universal Credit claim, contact Universal Credit.

Free independent debt advice and money guidance

You can get free, confidential and independent advice on a range of debt problems from debt advisors. Debt advisors can:

  • check to see if you're missing out on any benefits you could claim
  • help you deal with your debt by showing you options you might not know about
  • offer you tips to help you manage your money

Use the MoneyHelper tool to find an advisor near you or you can visit our webpage, get free debt advice, to find a list of organisations who can help with debt.

MoneyHelper also has useful tools on their website to help you manage your money, including:

  • Budget Planner which helps you build a budget, know exactly where money is being spent, and know what you've got coming in
  • Bill Prioritiser which helps you decide which payments to tackle first and, if you're struggling to pay, can tell you what to do before you miss a payment
