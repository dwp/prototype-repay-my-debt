Discovery: Communication Channels and Methods

  • Design ticket
Status
Ready for UR
Description
We currently lack a clear understanding of how customers experience and engage with debt-related communications across the repayment journey. Multiple channels are used, including letters, SMS, email and digital services, but there is limited evidence on how effectively these communications meet customer needs and support desired outcomes. This discovery will build an evidence base to understand communication effectiveness, identify user needs and pain points, and highlight opportunities for improvement.
